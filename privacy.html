Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This policy explains how [LEGAL ENTITY NAME] ("Unite") handles personal data on unite.market, in line with Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data (PDPL).

1. What we collect

  • Ticket details: name, email, phone number, university and ticket category.
  • Payment: handled by Blend and Stripe. We receive the payment status, amount and the last 4 digits of the card only. We never receive or store full card numbers.
  • At the door: your ticket scan and check-in time. ID documents are checked visually and are not photographed, copied or stored.
  • Website: basic technical logs (IP address, browser) kept by our hosting provider for security.

2. Why we use it

  • To sell and deliver your ticket, send you the venue address and Event updates, and run entry (performance of our contract with you).
  • To prevent fraud and keep the Event safe, and to meet legal and tax obligations.
  • To tell you about future Unite events, only if you opted in. You can unsubscribe at any time.

3. Who we share it with

Blend (ticketing), Stripe (payments), our hosting and email providers, and authorities where the law requires. We do not sell personal data. Some providers process data outside the UAE under appropriate safeguards.

4. How long we keep it

Ticket and check-in records are kept for 12 months after the Event, and payment records for as long as UAE law requires (generally 5 years). Then they are deleted.

5. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to marketing, by emailing support@unite.market. We reply within 30 days.
