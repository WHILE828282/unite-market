Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

1. On unite.market

You can browse this site without an account. We don't ask for your name, email or card here. Our hosting provider keeps standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser) to keep the site running and secure.

2. When you buy a ticket

Tickets are bought on the organiser's page (Upper Deck DXB, through Ticket Tailor). The details you enter there, including your payment, are collected by the organiser and their ticketing and payment providers under their own privacy policies. The organiser may tell us how many tickets were sold through our referral link, but not your card details.

3. Signing up on uniteuow.com

If you sign up on uniteuow.com (for example, to enter the VIP table giveaway), we keep your university email, your name and your entry, to run the giveaway and contact the winner. We don't sell your data.

4. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data at support@uniteuow.com. We follow the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021).
