About this event

Remember the movie? The house nobody was supposed to find, the music you could hear three streets away, red cups everywhere and a night nobody forgot. That’s the energy we’re bringing back.

For one night a private villa in Dubai turns into a real American house party. Red cups, party games on the terrace, lasers cutting through the smoke and a pool that won’t stay calm for long. No dress code, no club rules, just the party you’ve only seen in films.

The DJ plays it like a college house party in 2012: Kid Cudi, LMFAO, Flo Rida, 50 Cent and every throwback you know the words to, mixed with what’s hot right now. Loud from the first track to the last.

Students from AUD, Middlesex, Heriot-Watt, UOWD and more, all under one roof. All you need is your ticket and your crew.
