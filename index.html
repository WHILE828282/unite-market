About

Remember the movie? The house nobody was supposed to find, the music you could hear three streets away, red cups everywhere and a night nobody forgot. That’s the energy we’re bringing back.

For one night a private villa in JVC turns into a proper American house party: a live DJ, lasers and smoke, a pool, beer pong, and red cups in every hand. No dress code, no VIP-table nonsense, just the party you’ve only seen in films.

Students from AUD, Middlesex, Heriot-Watt, UOWD and more, all under one roof. Drinks and food are on us all night, so all you need is your ticket and your crew.

It started as a joke in a group chat: what if Dubai had its own Project X? One villa, no club rules, every uni invited. The chat got bigger, someone found the villa, and now it’s happening.

We want the chaos from the film, minus the flamethrower. Nobody gets the address until the day. If you know, you know.
