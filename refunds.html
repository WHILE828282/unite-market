Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026 · Applies to all tickets bought on unite.market

1. All sales are final

Tickets are non-refundable and non-exchangeable, except where the Event is cancelled by the organisers or as set out below. This includes, without limitation: change of plans, illness, travel, missing the last entry time (21:00), losing your ticket email, or not being able to attend for any other personal reason.

If you cannot attend, you may transfer your ticket to another person aged 21+ free of charge by emailing support@unite.market up to 48 hours before the Event.

2. Event cancelled by the organisers

  • If Unite cancels the Event for any reason, including at the request of an authority, you receive a full refund of the ticket price and any booking fee, automatically, to the original payment method.
  • We email every ticket holder as soon as a cancellation is decided. Refunds are initiated within 7 days of the cancellation; your bank may take a further 5 to 10 business days to show the money.
  • Unite is not responsible for travel, accommodation, beverages or other costs you incur in connection with the Event.

3. Postponement or material change

  • If the Event is postponed, your ticket stays valid for the new date. If you cannot attend the new date, you may request a full refund within 7 days of our postponement email.
  • If the Event moves outside Dubai or its start time moves by more than 3 hours, you may request a full refund within 7 days of our change email.

4. Refused entry: no refund

You will be refused entry or removed, and no refund will be given, in any of these cases:

  • Any attempt to enter with a fake, altered, borrowed, expired or someone else's ID, or with a photo, photocopy, screenshot or digital copy of an ID, results in immediate denial of entry with no refund. Fraudulent documents may be confiscated and reported to Dubai Police.
  • You are under 21, or cannot prove you are 21 or over with an original physical ID.
  • The name on your ticket does not match your ID and the ticket was not transferred through support.
  • You arrive after last entry (21:00), or your ticket has already been scanned.
  • You are visibly intoxicated, refuse a bag search, carry prohibited items, or break the Terms & Conditions or UAE law.
  • The ticket was resold, counterfeit, or subject to a chargeback.

5. Payment errors

If you were charged twice, charged the wrong amount, or charged without receiving a ticket, email us with your payment receipt within 30 days. Verified errors are refunded in full to the original payment method.

6. How to request a refund

Email support@unite.market from the address used to buy the ticket, with your order number and the reason. We reply within 2 business days. Approved refunds are made only to the original payment method, in AED.

7. Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy affects your rights under Federal Law No. 15 of 2020 on Consumer Protection and its implementing regulations.
